Introduction

Global trade data is highly fragmented. Pakistani traders currently lack access to structured, actionable global trade intelligence, forcing them to rely on manual, opaque processes for deal discovery and competitive benchmarking.

ZaraiLink solves this by providing a unified cross-border trade intelligence platform that connects Pakistani traders with global buyers and suppliers, and helps international traders discover Pakistani counterparts across all trade sectors.

Objectives

  • AI Trade Search Engine: Accept natural language queries, plain product names, or HS codes to instantly locate trade counterparts.
  • NLU Pipeline: Build a custom Natural Language Understanding pipeline to extract intent (buy/sell), products, prices, and locations.
  • Deal Intelligence: Provide deep analytics on market pricing, shipment volumes, and competitive positioning.
  • Supplier Comparison: Side-by-side benchmarking of up to 4 entities with visual metrics and export capabilities.
  • Trade Directory: A searchable, verified directory of Pakistani trading companies.
  • Token Paywall: Implement a sustainable token-based access control system for premium data unlocking.

Conclusion & Future Work

ZaraiLink successfully bridges the gap in cross-border trade intelligence for Pakistan by utilizing a specialized 4-stage NLU pipeline to extract actionable parameters from natural language. By mitigating latency through conditional LLM triggering, the platform delivers near real-time insights.

Future Work:

  • Expansion of the SetFit training dataset to cover multilingual queries (e.g., Urdu/Roman Urdu).
  • Integration of predictive pricing models using historical shipment data.
  • Automated matching engine to proactively suggest trade partners based on user profiles.
